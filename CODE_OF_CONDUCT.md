# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We aim to maintain a respectful, welcoming, and constructive environment for everyone working on or discussing Pipesor, regardless of experience, background, or identity.

## Expected behavior

- Communicate respectfully and assume good faith while challenging ideas constructively.
- Provide actionable feedback about code, designs, and decisions, not personal attacks.
- Respect privacy, boundaries, and confidential project information.
- Give credit for contributions and acknowledge mistakes transparently.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, intimidation, targeted insults, unwanted sexual attention, disclosure of private information without permission, or sustained disruptive conduct are not tolerated.

## Scope

These expectations apply to Pipesor's GitHub repositories, issue discussions, pull requests, reviews, and project-related collaboration spaces.

Maintainers will review reports confidentially to the extent reasonably possible, listen to affected parties, and may warn, restrict, or remove participants according to the severity and context of the behavior. Reports will not be publicly disclosed without a compelling reason and appropriate consideration for those involved.

This is a project-specific policy, not a verbatim copy of the Contributor Covenant.
